# Official Sources

Check these sources at task time because platform permissions and rules change.

## Core platform

- [Douyin user service agreement](https://www.douyin.com/draft/douyin_agreement/douyin_agreement_user.html?id=6773906068725565448) — current user obligations, platform rules, prohibited technical interference, and enforcement terms.
- [Douyin Open Platform overview](https://open.douyin.com/platform/resource/docs/accession-guide/platform-introduction/) — official platform and supported solution categories.
- [Video data access solution](https://open.douyin.com/platform/resource/docs/ability/open-data/video-data-solution) — authorized access to official video and account statistics.
- [Open Platform permissions](https://open.douyin.com/platform/resource/docs/accession-guide/type-and-permission) — current data and content scopes and their approval requirements.

## Ecommerce

- [Douyin ecommerce official learning platform](https://school.jinritemai.com/doudian/web/home?home_version=new) — current creator, merchant, content, traffic, advertising, and compliance courses.

## Operating rule

Use the in-product creator center and rules center as the primary source for the signed-in account. Do not encode a third-party list of “sensitive words,” distribution tricks, or universal metric thresholds as platform fact. For AIGC, regulated claims, ecommerce, local services, ads, or live content, recheck the current applicable rule before finalizing the brief.
