# Video Production Workflow

Use this workflow after an operating review identifies the audience problem, funnel stage, and experiment variable. Keep every artifact inside one project folder so the final result remains traceable to its brief and measurement plan.

## 1. Brief gate

Complete `01-brief.md` before scripting:

- one audience problem and one promised outcome
- one funnel stage and primary metric
- one changed variable and controlled elements
- proof the video can honestly show
- one CTA mapped to the business path
- target duration band and production mode
- success threshold and comparison set

Stop when the promise needs unavailable evidence, an unapproved claim, or an unowned asset.

## 2. Script gate

Complete `02-script.md` as timed beats rather than a prose essay:

1. Open with the problem, result, conflict, or proof.
2. Establish context only after the hook earns attention.
3. Show evidence before expanding the explanation.
4. Remove repeated setup, greetings, and abstract filler.
5. End with one CTA; do not stack follow, comment, message, and purchase asks.

Read the spoken script aloud. Revise sentences that are difficult to say naturally or exceed the configured duration budget. Lock the script before recording unless the chosen mode intentionally allows a documented improvisation section.

## 3. Asset gate

Complete `03-shot-list.md`. Give every shot a stable ID and record:

- purpose and corresponding script beat
- source: camera, screen recording, product capture, archive, generated, or licensed
- framing, action, duration, and required text
- file path and status
- rights, likeness, music, trademark, and disclosure notes

Use real product or process proof when the promise depends on real behavior. Do not let decorative B-roll substitute for evidence.

## 4. Capture gate

Capture clean source material before editing:

- record a clean master take plus targeted pickups
- keep screen recordings readable at the delivery crop
- capture before/after material under comparable conditions
- preserve room tone or clean dialogue when useful
- name source files with shot IDs
- keep originals separate from selected takes and exports

For AI-assisted material, preserve the source, generation method, and disclosure requirement. Do not synthesize a person's likeness or voice without authorization.

## 5. Edit gate

Complete `04-edit-plan.md` in passes:

1. **Structure cut**: arrange hook, proof, explanation, and CTA.
2. **Pacing pass**: remove dead air and repeated meaning; keep necessary comprehension time.
3. **Picture lock**: stop changing structure before caption and audio finishing.
4. **Caption pass**: match speech, preserve meaning, check names and numbers, and keep text inside safe zones.
5. **Audio pass**: prioritize intelligible dialogue, check transitions, and verify music rights.
6. **Graphics and disclosure pass**: add only information that improves comprehension or satisfies policy.

Do not use motion, cuts, captions, or sound effects merely to simulate energy. Each treatment must clarify, prove, or pace the story.

## 6. Export QA gate

Use the configured canvas and export defaults as production defaults, not claims about permanent platform requirements.

Inspect the actual exported file:

- open and play it from start to finish
- inspect the first frame and first three seconds
- sample the configured number of middle frames
- inspect the final frame and CTA
- verify orientation, crop, safe zones, caption legibility, audio sync, and black frames
- verify product facts, numbers, before/after comparability, AIGC labels, and rights
- confirm the filename and publish package point to the reviewed export

If any check fails, return to the relevant gate and export again.

## 7. Publish package and measurement

Complete `05-publish-pack.md` with the reviewed file, caption, cover or first-frame choice, one CTA, topics, disclosures, and final approval status. Publishing remains a separate external action requiring explicit confirmation.

After the configured review window, complete `06-review.md` with source, date range, funnel metrics, confounders, verdict, and the next single-variable experiment. Preserve failed experiments; they are evidence, not clutter.
